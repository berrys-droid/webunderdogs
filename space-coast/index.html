How a local read works

The same four steps as the wine work.

  1. Map the prompts

    Ten questions your customers really ask, built with you, then frozen so the comparison stays fair.

  2. Take the baseline

    Four runs per prompt on ChatGPT, Google's AI answers, Gemini and Claude. Named, cited and described correctly, or not.

  3. Fix what they read

    Your Google Business Profile, your listings and your service pages, answer first and in agreement with each other.

  4. Run it again

    Same prompts, 28 days or more later, including what didn't move.
